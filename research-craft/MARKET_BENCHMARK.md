## Capability-to-source map

- Problem framing and hypothesis discipline: K-Dense `hypothesis-generation` plus the existing evidence-state model.
- Experiment design: K-Dense `experimental-design`; adapted experimental-unit, pseudoreplication, nuisance-factor, randomization, blocking, interaction, and design-readiness rules.
- Diagnosis and software problem solving: Superpowers `systematic-debugging`, `test-driven-development`, and `verification-before-completion`.
- Statistical reasoning: K-Dense `statistical-analysis`; adapted design-aware selection, diagnostics, effect/uncertainty, practical threshold, equivalence, Bayesian, and multiplicity gates.
- Data quality and analysis audit: K-Dense `exploratory-data-analysis` and `scientific-critical-thinking`; adapted manifest-first inspection, non-destructive transformations, severity, and actionable findings.
- Literature/frontier work: K-Dense `literature-review` and `hypothesis-generation`; adapted pilot/frozen searches, staged screening, common extraction, source-bound novelty, and thematic synthesis.
- Scientific writing and reviewer response: K-Dense `scientific-writing` and `peer-review`; adapted evidence outlines, no-fabrication fields, methods/results reconciliation, confidentiality, proportional requests, and response-to-change closure.
- Scientific plots: K-Dense `scientific-visualization` plus Anthropic's artifact inspection pattern; adapted explicit export and rendered final-size QA.
- Scientific diagrams: direct MIT vendoring from Bybit, ML Architecture Diagram, Agents365 draw.io, and FigureSpec; adapted K-Dense/Anthropic QA plus Scientific Illustrator's checkpointed editable reconstruction and AgentFigureGallery's bounded reference-selection pattern.
- ML/CV, systems performance, security/privacy, and RAG/LLM: existing domain gates strengthened with K-Dense's experimental/measurement discipline and corroborating performance, security, MLOps, and LLM evaluation patterns from `wshobson/agents`; no vendor-specific orchestration copied.
- Completion, organization, and reproducibility: Superpowers fresh-evidence verification integrated with existing registries, manifests, state, and quality gates.

The runtime remains one Skill. No Suite, Router, Handoff, plugin orchestration, workspace-schema change, telemetry, or mandatory third-party service was introduced.
