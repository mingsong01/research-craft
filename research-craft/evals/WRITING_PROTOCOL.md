# Content-level writing evaluation

`writing_cases.json` contains eight anonymous synthetic development tasks, one per
requested writing capability. The numbers are invented fixture data, not research results.
Do not load this file during ordinary manuscript work or treat it as factual guidance.

For an evaluated run, give only the case instructions/materials and the selected Skill
version. Preserve the actual diagnosis, revised passage/response, cited source locations,
unresolved facts and artifact diff. A proposed change must not appear as completed work.
Freeze the model/effort/tools and evidence packet across versions. Do not show the rubric
or answer key to the evaluated run. Use an isolated workspace and an independent grader
when available; no paid call or new agent is implied by this protocol.

Grade the **delivered content**, separately on:

1. Scientific correctness: does the repair actually address the supplied operation,
   comparison or inference? Check numbers, unfavorable observations and alternative causes.
2. Argument quality: does the gap support the design, the paragraph have a coherent job,
   and the response answer every substantive part rather than an easier substitute?
3. Evidence/claim calibration: are scope, novelty, causality, uncertainty and source status
   supported? Preserve warranted strong claims and bounded positive controls.
4. Language and bilingual meaning: inspect propositions, terminology, negation,
   quantifiers, conditions and units; judge natural expression after fidelity.
5. Action/version consistency: can an actual change be located, are planned experiments
   distinct from executed ones, and do the manuscript and reply refer to the same facts?

For each applicable dimension, record ACCEPT / PARTIAL / REJECT and a specific passage
or omitted demand. No numeric composite is required. Reject for invented data/citations,
semantic reversal, hidden unfavorable results, unsupported experiment-completed claims,
unverified speaker attribution or incompatible numerical/protocol comparisons.
The presence of terms such as gap, uncertainty or reviewer is never a scoring rule.
Correcting grammar while leaving the scientific defect is not success; unnecessarily
rewriting accurate prose or making every claim weak is also a failure.

Real manuscript comparisons need exact version/source binding, historical before/after
separate from newly proposed text, and an independent source check. They test whether
the diagnosis and revision are defensible, not whether one Skill caused a published edit.

The phase-2B maintainer validation records actual local-source cases and same-author
development responses outside the distributable Skill. A separate eight-category holdout
packet is frozen there, excluded from instructions and unexecuted in this stage. It is
fresh synthetic material, but not certified unseen to its author or evaluated blind.
Do not present these self-reviews, schema validation or existing contract checks as
independent model-quality improvements. Run the holdout only with explicit authorization
for the chosen execution method, keeping keys withheld and reporting regressions.
